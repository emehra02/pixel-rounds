### 3. Kawasaki disease

1. 3-year-old boy with a fever for 6 days that is not responding to antibiotics.
2. Bilateral conjunctival injection without discharge.
3. Red, cracked lips and a strawberry tongue.
4. Swollen hands and feet; fingertips later peel.
